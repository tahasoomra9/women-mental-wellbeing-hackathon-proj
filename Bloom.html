Weekly summary

A week can be hard to put into words.

Bloom pulls together what she talked about in her chats. She can look back at how she felt each day, how much sleep she got and how often she woke up.

Mum’s wellbeing

How has mum been feeling?

Five hours of sleep can feel very different when it comes in short stretches. Bloom helps her keep track of details like that, so she has something to go on when someone asks how she’s doing.

How are you feeling today?

She can record her mood, how supported she feels and how much she slept, then add a note.

What’s changed this week?

Bloom brings her check-ins and chat themes together in a seven-day view. An optional wellbeing score shows what went into it.

This week · Evening support

“You mentioned that the evenings felt easier when your sister was there.”

Could I use more support?

The Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale asks ten questions about the past week. Her answers can help her decide what to discuss with a health professional.

Postnatal mood questionnaire

A screening result can start a conversation. A clinician makes a diagnosis.

What do I want to tell my GP?

She picks a week to see her mood, sleep, screening results and points from her chats. She can edit the talking points and questions, then download a PDF.

A question for my appointment

“Even when someone else has the baby, I struggle to switch off. What might help?”
